Installing

Android The app isn't from Google Play, so Android asks first. Open the downloaded file, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
Windows If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway. This appears because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate.

What it does

  • Finds network scanners automatically (eSCL and HP)
  • Rotate, reorder and delete scanned pages
  • Save as PDF, searchable PDF, JPEG or PNG
  • Merge PDFs and images into one PDF
  • English, Swedish and Arabic
  • Light and dark theme
